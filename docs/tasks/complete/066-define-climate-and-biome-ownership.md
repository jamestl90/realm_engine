# Define Climate And Biome Ownership

Status: complete
Priority: high
Area: Procgen / Greater Realm Architecture

## Goal

Define the engine boundary between stable grand-scale environmental tendencies, biome assignment, and time-varying runtime weather before adding any new greater-realm climate or biome fields.

## Context

Task 028 generated rainfall, humidity, and moisture during map generation. Task 039 removed those fields because weather events, runoff, and active discharge belong outside stable procgen output.

The project therefore needs an explicit contract for stable greater-realm climate normals or biome inputs, including whether the reusable engine exports generic environmental factors while applications supply biome definitions. This decision must preserve the established rule that procgen does not predetermine weather events and must not require local-region generation.

## Dependencies

- Task 039: separation of generated drainage from runtime weather.
- Task 064: complete stable water classification.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Decide whether stable greater-realm climate normals or environmental tendency fields belong in procgen, world simulation, or application data.
- Decide whether biome labels are engine-owned, application-owned, or produced by an engine classifier from application-supplied rules.
- Define the minimum reusable inputs and outputs without reintroducing generated precipitation events, transient humidity, soil moisture, runoff, or discharge.
- Define how elevation, latitude, coast distance, terrain form, and inland water may influence stable tendencies, with parked watershed metadata treated only as a possible future input.
- Define determinism, serialization, and dependency-aware greater-realm regeneration expectations.
- Record the dependency direction between greater-realm generation, biome data, and future local-world work without requiring region or chunk systems now.
- Update `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `docs/PROCGEN.md` where the decision changes their contracts.
- Create focused implementation tasks for the approved climate-normal and biome slices; do not implement those slices in this task.

## Decisions

- Stable climatological normals belong to greater-realm procgen because they are deterministic world geography derived from terrain and explicit generation settings, not time-varying simulation state.
- The minimum climate outputs are `temperature_normal` and `precipitation_normal`, each using fixed `0..1` semantic anchors that remain comparable across maps. Outputs must not be renormalized to each map's observed minimum and maximum.
- Climate output lives in a separate `GreaterRealmClimateMap` with the same dimensions as its source `GreaterRealmMap`. Climate values do not return to `GreaterRealmCell` and do not affect terrain, classification, drainage, or potential river channels.
- Temperature consumes explicit north/south latitude context, elevation, water proximity for maritime moderation, and optional broad deterministic variation.
- Precipitation normal represents long-term relative precipitation tendency. It may consume ocean and inland-water moisture sources, prevailing-wind settings, elevation, and orographic lift/rain-shadow behavior.
- Generated humidity, soil moisture, current rainfall, runoff, and river discharge remain forbidden procgen outputs. Task 028's superseded fields are not restored under new names.
- Biome definitions and labels are application-owned. The engine may provide a reusable deterministic evaluator that consumes application-supplied rules and emits opaque application-provided biome IDs into a separate `GreaterRealmBiomeMap`.
- Biome rules may inspect terrain form, water form, elevation, slope, coast distance, temperature normal, and precipitation normal. Applications separately own names, colours, art, resources, and gameplay meaning.
- Terrain changes invalidate climate and biome output. Climate-setting changes invalidate climate and biome output only. Biome-rule changes invalidate biome output only. Debug-view changes rebuild visualization only.
- Generated climate and biome arrays are regenerated by default rather than persisted. Saved inputs must carry explicit climate-algorithm and biome-rule-set versions before durable caches are introduced.
- Future local generation may sample greater-realm climate and biome output, but Tasks 067-073 are not prerequisites for generating or viewing these grand-scale layers.
- Future gameplay simulation may consume climate normals and biome assignments as probability inputs later, but it cannot mutate generated terrain, climate normals, or biome assignments.
